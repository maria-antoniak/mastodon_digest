Two reports into aerial atrocities vis a vis the conflict in Ethiopia.

Firstly, HRW conducted a visual probe of an Ethiopian airstrike on the Tigrayan capital of Mekele in late August. It was one of a slew of strikes in the lead up to war breaking out Tuesday. 13 civilians were injured. HRW found no evidence of any valid military targets. A residential area was targeted, HRW say it was an indiscriminate strike and a possible war crime.
